Bragging Rights

Three runs a day. Same dungeons for everyone. Best haul wins.

    Playtest

    Unlimited runs on random dungeons. They never count towards daily scores or records.

    v0 prototype: scores stay on this phone and the play limit is on your honour.

    How to play

    • Tap a neighbouring square to move, in any of 8 directions. Tap an enemy next to you to attack it.
    • Red squares are where enemies will strike next turn. Step out of them. Hold an enemy to see what it does.
    • Hitting first matters: you always act before enemies do.
    • Walking away costs. When a goblin or wraith next to you is winding up, its reach is outlined. Step out of the outline and it gets a free hit. Kill it, sidestep within its reach, or pay the heart.
    • Hearts are your health. You get one back each time you reach the stairs.
    • Your lamp burns a little every turn. When it goes out, the run ends. Pick up oil to top it up.
    • Pages bend the rules once, and crumble when you leave the floor. Use them.
    • The ring hides you for a few turns, but every use draws a wraith, now and on every floor after.
    • At the stairs, bank your relics and leave, or go deeper for more. Die or run out of oil and you keep only a quarter.
    • 8 floors. Three runs a day, and all three count towards your daily total.
