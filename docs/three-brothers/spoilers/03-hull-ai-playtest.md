# Chapter 3 AI playtest walkthrough

**SPOILERS. Play the chapter first.** This replaces the walkthrough of the first version, which was rewritten after Jack's playtest.

## The route

- **Pub thread:** salvage the soapbox (plank and bent strap). Stamp the strap flat on the plank. Lever the trapdoor in Ye Olde White Harte with it. In the dark cellar, use the phone's torch. Lift the fallen barrel; the landlord pays half a crown.
- **Paper thread:** the editor hires Rob by the line and gives him a notebook. At the Boulevard the wall has glass on top: lay the plank along it, then write on it (Rob is tall enough without a box). He misses the goal. The boy describes the scorer (a bandage) for some dolly mixture. The woman on the bench says her Albert plays up front with six stitches in his head. The pub's team photo has two A.s; the centre forward is A. Coultas. Name him to the editor: half a crown and a late edition.
- **Book thread:** the late edition goes to the woman, who gives Rob her Dracula. In it, old Swales on the Whitby bench talks like Phil: "young man", Knaresborough, the well.
- **Ticket:** two half crowns cover the 4s 6d to Knaresborough, but Rob won't choose it until he's read the book.

## Lore for later authors

Phil got himself into Dracula: Stoker (Chapter 1, the churchyard bench) gave old Mr Swales Phil's way of talking, and a line about getting to the well at Knaresborough. Rob has the copy and a ticket to Knaresborough, by way of York.

## What the rounds found

- Best puzzle every round: the scorer deduction (three sources across three scenes, with a decoy).
- Best jokes every round: the barrel nobody saw, missing the goal while drawing the left-back, the by-law about being tall, "Medium, then", and the editor's cuts.
- Recurring problems, each fixed: refusals that named the fix, Rob announcing his own traits, word-for-word repeats, a needed item hidden in the world after use, and hints that went stale.
- Flags that only vary wording multiplied the solver's states (up to 77,000). Trimmed back to about 20,000.
