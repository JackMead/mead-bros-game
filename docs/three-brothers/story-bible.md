# Story bible

The shared memory of the story. Every chapter's author reads this first and updates it after. Claude keeps it in step when building a chapter.

It only records what a player learns by finishing a chapter, never how its puzzles are solved. Still, play the chapters before reading the lore for them.

## Premise

The three Mead brothers visit Mother Shipton's Cave in Knaresborough for a day out. Their selfie in front of the Petrifying Well catches Mother Shipton herself. They won't let her in the photo ("brothers only"), so she curses them:

> Three that would not share the frame
> through Yorkshire's years shall scatter wide.
> No county bound ye cross, no home ye claim,
> till three again stand side by side.

## The rules of the world

These hold in every chapter. Change one only on purpose, and record it under Lore.

1. **Scattered through time.** Each brother is somewhere (somewhen) different in Yorkshire's past, or future.
2. **Never leaving Yorkshire.** Nobody can cross the county boundary. Every chapter is set in Yorkshire.
3. **The way home:** all three standing side by side. Where and when is for the chapters to discover.
4. **The Petrifying Well is the link between times.** It has been turning things to stone since before anyone wrote it down. Something dropped in the well in one era can turn up, petrified, in a later one. This is how brothers can help each other across time.
5. **The finale** (back together in the present) is written by whoever writes the last chapter, using only what earlier chapters established.

## Lore established

Facts later chapters must respect.

- The curse (above), word for word.
- Mother Shipton stepped out of her own statue as a glowing purple spirit. She can stop the water of the well mid-air.
- At the moment of the curse, the petrified teddy bears on the well turned their heads, eyes glowing red.
- The curse opened a time vortex in the Petrifying Well. It pulled the brothers in one by one, and three streaks of light shot off in different directions.
- A phone was left lying on the gravel by the well, still showing the selfie.
- The selfie was taken, with Mother Shipton's face in the background.

## Open threads

Things a later chapter can pick up. Close them by moving them to "Closed threads" with the chapter that did it. Part of the fun is learning which to close and which to leave open.

- Where did each brother land, and when?
- Who finds the phone with the selfie on it, and when? Does the photo still show her face?
- Why did the teddy bears turn their heads?
- Does Mother Shipton want something besides being in the photo?
- Rob's soapbox: did it travel with him?
