# Chapter 3 AI playtest walkthrough

**SPOILERS. Play the chapter first.**

## The route

1. Salvage the wrecked soapbox for the lid and iron strap. Hook the rosette off King Billy with the strap, and wear it to get into the committee rooms.
2. Fail the barbell to learn about grip. Trade half the dolly mixture to the sugar-starved billiards player for chalk. Chalk up and win two shillings.
3. Buy Dracula (sixpence) and read the note at the back. Buy the Wensleydale (a shilling) for its crate.
4. Lid plus crate rebuilds the soapbox. Rant the cyclists off the road, and pick up the cap one of them drops.
5. At the Boulevard, the low sun is blinding the goalkeeper: give him the cap. Then the crowd is calm, so give Lloyd George the soapbox and get a rail warrant and the lid back.
6. Warrant to the clerk, then the train to Knaresborough. Give the warden the cheese to read the ledger. "Mr Y. Mann" is Phil's "young man": lever hook 9 with the strap. Scratch a message on the lid with the strap and hang it on the empty hook for the last sixpence.

## Round 1 findings

- **Praised:** the Y. Mann deduction, the rant at the cyclists, handing over the soapbox, the train refrain, the ending.
- **Too easy:** chalk was stated and free, the keeper's own line named the fix, the warden named the cheese, the parcel refusal named the lever.
- **Bugs:** the barbell denied having chalk while holding it; the sun's position contradicted itself; the clerk named Knaresborough before the note; stale purse and lid text; the Goal target resolved to the goalkeeper; the note could be missed by a player who never looked at the book; the plank's thirty words were never shown.
- **Changes:** chalk now costs half the dolly mixture and needs a clue connected; clues cut as above; the note can also be read by using the book on Rob; the plank shows its message.

## Round 2 findings

- Veteran 2.5/5, Button-Masher 2.5/5. No stuck points. Y. Mann is still the best moment.
- The parcel can be enumerated with no penalty, though the warden names the owner each time, which keeps the joke working.
- Fixed afterwards: the warrant's stale fallback line and description, the warden's parcel line, and softer crate and billiards clues.
