Round 1, 2026-10-29. Full reports from each tester profile.

## The Veteran

Finished in 49 commands (about 20 spent looking around first). Difficulty 1.5/5, "very fair".

- Dog on the selfie spot: meal deal via Jack, pie to the dog. Clues: dog eyeing the kiosk, the board maths, Jack's £2.20, Jack's vegetarianism. Reasoned, not brute-forced. ~8 commands.
- Camera: Phil's phone ("only one with battery"). ~3 commands.
- Cold fingers: tea to Phil. Clues: Phil's description, the tea's "warming your hands" line. ~4 commands.
- Arms too short: phone to Rob ("someone taller"). ~3 commands.
- Not high enough: soapbox on the selfie spot. ~2 commands.

Issues raised:
- Talking to a brother ("use jack") gives a generic quip, never a hint or reaction.
- Unlocking the phone takes one use, then a second use to try the photo.
- Rob refusing the pork pie as "poor value" is odd when he has no cash anyway.
- Switching brother for every hand-off could get tedious in a long chapter.

Funny: the minus-fifty-pence pie, Maureen's "I just work here", Phil downing the tea, the soapbox rant, the photobomb, the curse.

Finished in 45 commands. Difficulty 2/5, fair, "never felt stuck".

- Dog: theories about the statue and dropping things in the well first (~8 commands, deliberately); drooling dog led to food. Reasoned. ~20 commands total.
- Cold fingers: tried warming hands in the well (nicely refused). Tea reuse felt satisfying. ~6 commands.

## Summary of round 1

All four finished; nobody brute-forced; ratings 1.5 to 2 out of 5 (brief asked for Gentle). No puzzle flagged as unfair or stuck.

Fixes made after round 1:
- Talking to a brother now gets a reply from him, with a hint where it helps (Jack mentions cash, Phil his frozen fingers).
- In the game: tap a brother in the scene to talk, his portrait to play as him. The intro (and the text version's intro) says so.
- Wallet on the kiosk opens the order; the lone pork pie refusal now depends on who has the cash.
- The selfie spot's line changes once the phone is unlocked; more varied soapbox refusals.
- Phil's fallback lines no longer assume cold hands.
- Text mode: "use <item>" on its own asks "on what?".
