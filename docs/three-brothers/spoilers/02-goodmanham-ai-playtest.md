# Chapter 2, The Mead Hall: AI playtests (SPOILERS)

Play the chapter before reading this.

## Round 1 (12 puzzles, shortest route 27 moves)

### The Veteran: finished, 122 commands, 2.5/5

Solved everything with no brute force, but "the chapter tells you the answer too often". Per obstacle: Ecca's water (1 try); Wulf's gift (coin refused, full bottle refused with "bring it him empty", which gave the answer); hearth stick (trivial, the look text pointed at spare sticks); barrow with the stick (1 command, Ecca's line said a spear was inside); photo on the platter (2 commands, best clue in the chapter); boar to the cat (1 command: Jack's "make it want to be somewhere else" gave it away); lot to Coifi (obvious after four characters said "always stay"); west door bar and carabiner (2 commands, the two rings made it clear); spearhead on bar (1 command; the idols refusal said outright Coifi throws a spear at the temple); barley trail (first guess; Jack said "it needs a sparrow"); mare to the stallion (groom said "down to the river").

Bugs: Gytha repeated her whole first speech; no river hotspot; "Hygiene hasn't been invented" line awkward. Estimated 15 to 20 minutes for an experienced human. Suggested: cut answers from refusals, add a binding step to the spear, make the torch go out, make the stallion's whereabouts an inference.

### The Button-Masher: gave up at the 90-command cap, 7 of 12 solved, 3/5

Wulf's gift fell to cycling the inventory (about 5 tries). The meat chain resisted a little; the sparrow puzzle resisted brute force best. Bugs: barley refusal said "Ecca said..." when he hadn't; looking at the wallet added items without saying so; no refusal for filling the bottle at the river; the heron wasn't in the scene list; the door state after lifting the bar was unclear. Lots of walking between scenes.

### Changes after round 1

- The burning stick blows out on the Wolds and must be relit in Gytha's empty hut (new step).
- The spearhead is loose on the bar and must be lashed with the groom's rope, which only comes free once the stallion is back (new step; ties the two late threads together).
- Removed answer-giving lines: the cat, Osric/sparrow, the idols, "bring it him empty", the groom's "down to the river" (Ecca now carries that clue alone). Ecca and Gytha no longer repeat the "always stay" pattern; Coifi and Paulinus carry it.
- Fixed: Gytha's repeat speech, river hotspot, wallet message, barley refusal, heron in the description, door state line, awkward line. Cat now visible from the start.

## Round 3 (16 puzzles, shortest route 38 moves)

### Changes after round 3 (not retested)

- Wulf now only says rawhide must be "bound on and shrunk tight"; the groom (shown the strap) explains that hide softens when soaked and shrinks by a fire. The method is now split across two characters.
- The groom mentions the old halter on the post once the stallion is back.
- Fixed Ecca's stale description, a specific refusal at the spit, and a hint at the barrow.

### Where it stands

Three rounds moved the Veteran from 2.5 to 2 to 2.5. Each round added steps and cut signposting, but the Veteran's main point held: almost every step has one obvious candidate, so a methodical player is never stuck. Reaching 4 would need a different structure (several plausible uses for each item, more decoys, needs that are never stated), which is a bigger rework than a polish pass.
