# Prologue: The Selfie

**Author:** Rob
**Era and place:** Present day. Mother Shipton's Cave, Knaresborough
**Spotlight brother(s):** All three

## What happens

The three brothers have a day out at Mother Shipton's Cave and try to take a selfie in front of the Petrifying Well. Mother Shipton suddenly appears. Because they won't let her in the selfie, she casts them out across time, saying they can't leave the bounds of Yorkshire until they're together again.
